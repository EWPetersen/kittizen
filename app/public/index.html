Find your shard

  1. Join the Persistent Universe and wait until you are connected.
  2. Open the game console with the ~ / ` key, usually below Esc on a US keyboard. The key can differ on other layouts.
  3. Type r_displayinfo 1, press Enter, then close the console with the same key.
  4. In the debug overlay, usually at the top right, find the Shard / Shard ID information. If it is missing, try r_displayinfo 2 for the expanded overlay. Labels and positions can change with game updates.
  5. Choose the region you connected to: US, EU, AUS, or ASIA. Enter the last three digits of the shard ID, including leading zeros. For example, a shard ID ending in 007 in EU becomes EU-007 here.

Use the shard identifier, rather than the build number, session ID, player count, or individual server/authority ID.

To hide the overlay afterward, enter r_displayinfo 0.

Kittizen uses a shortened region + three-digit identifier. A suffix may be shared by different full shard IDs; this is not a guaranteed unique identifier.

Trust, with context

Only signed-in pilots can verify a report. You cannot verify your own report or vote twice.

Each verifier contributes once to an author's reputation, using their latest vote across that author's reports. Evidence halves in weight every 7 days. Disputes lower the score; no evidence gives a score of zero.

Score = 100 × weighted confirmations ÷ (weighted confirmations + weighted disputes + 4). Trusted requires 70 points and at least 10 weighted confirmations.

Local accounts are not verified RSI identities. Multiple accounts and coordinated voting remain possible; public launch needs stronger identity and abuse controls.
